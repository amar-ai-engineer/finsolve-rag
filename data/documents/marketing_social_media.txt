NexaFin Technologies - Social Media Guidelines

Platforms: Active presence on LinkedIn (primary), Twitter/X (secondary), and GitHub (developer community). Instagram and TikTok are not priorities for B2B fintech.

Posting Frequency: LinkedIn: 4 posts/week (2 company, 2 thought leadership). Twitter: daily (mix of industry commentary, product updates, and developer tips). GitHub: monthly open-source contributions and documentation updates.

Content Pillars:
1. Technical thought leadership (API design, payment security, compliance)
2. Customer success stories (anonymized unless client approves)
3. Engineering culture (team highlights, hackathon results, open source work)
4. Industry trends (embedded finance, BaaS, regulatory changes)

Approval Process: Routine posts (content calendar items) - Marketing team posts directly. Sensitive topics (pricing changes, partnerships, financial results) - require CMO approval. Crisis communications - only CEO or CMO may post.

Employee Social Media: Employees are encouraged to share company content. Always disclose your NexaFin affiliation. Never share unreleased product info, financial data, or customer details. When in doubt, check with Marketing before posting.

Response Protocol: All comments and DMs monitored during business hours. Customer complaints: acknowledge within 2 hours, escalate to support. Trolls and spam: do not engage, report and block. Media inquiries: forward to press@nexafin.com immediately.
