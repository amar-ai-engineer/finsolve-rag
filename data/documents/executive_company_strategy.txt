NexaFin Technologies - 3-Year Strategic Plan (2026-2028)
CONFIDENTIAL - Executive Access Only

Vision: Become the leading embedded payments platform for mid-market SaaS companies, processing $10B+ annually by 2028.

Year 1 (2026) - Foundation:
- Launch NexaPay 2.0 with multi-currency support (Q2)
- Reach $20M ARR by December 2026
- Expand to 500 active customers (currently 180)
- Close Series B at $25M+ valuation $150M+
- Hire to 85 employees (currently 52)
- Obtain European payment license (EMI)

Year 2 (2027) - Scale:
- Launch in UK and EU markets
- Reach $50M ARR
- Build partnership ecosystem (20+ integration partners)
- Explore acquisition of 1-2 complementary companies
- Reach 1,500 active customers
- Consider international office (London or Amsterdam)

Year 3 (2028) - Dominance:
- Process $10B+ in transactions annually
- Reach $100M ARR
- IPO preparation or Series C at $500M+ valuation
- Expand to LATAM and APAC markets
- 5,000+ active customers

Key Risks:
- Regulatory changes in payment processing (mitigation: dedicated compliance team)
- Stripe launching competing embedded product (mitigation: focus on mid-market, superior developer experience)
- Talent retention in competitive market (mitigation: equity refresh program, competitive salaries)

Exit Strategy: Preferred path is IPO in 2029. Backup: strategic acquisition by Visa, Mastercard, or major banking platform. Minimum acceptable acquisition price: $500M.
