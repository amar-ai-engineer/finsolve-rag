NexaFin Technologies - Employee Handbook (2026 Edition)

Welcome to NexaFin! This handbook covers the key policies every team member should know.

Working Hours: Standard hours are 9 AM to 6 PM, Monday through Friday. We offer flexible scheduling - you can shift your hours by up to 2 hours with manager approval (e.g., 7 AM - 4 PM). Remote work is available 2 days per week for all roles. Engineering has full remote flexibility.

Probation Period: All new hires complete a 90-day probation period. During this time, either party may terminate with 2 weeks notice. After probation, the standard notice period is 30 days for employees and 60 days for the company.

Performance Reviews: Conducted quarterly. Each review includes self-assessment, peer feedback (2 peers minimum), and manager evaluation. Ratings: Exceeds Expectations, Meets Expectations, Needs Improvement. Two consecutive "Needs Improvement" ratings trigger a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP).

Benefits: Health insurance (company covers 80%), dental and vision (company covers 60%), 401K matching up to 4%, annual learning budget of $2,000 per employee, gym membership reimbursement up to $100/month.

Code of Conduct: Zero tolerance for harassment, discrimination, or retaliation. Report concerns to HR directly or through the anonymous ethics hotline at ethics@nexafin.com. All reports are investigated within 5 business days.
