NexaFin Technologies - Investment and Treasury Policy

Cash Reserves: The company maintains a minimum of $3M in liquid reserves at all times. Excess cash above the $3M threshold may be invested in low-risk instruments approved by the CFO and board.

Approved Investment Vehicles:
- US Treasury Bills (T-Bills) - up to 60% of investable funds
- Money market funds (AAA-rated only) - up to 40%
- Corporate bonds (investment grade, A-rated minimum) - up to 20%
- No cryptocurrency, derivatives, or equity investments permitted

Foreign Exchange: All international transactions must be hedged if exceeding $50K. Approved hedging instruments include forward contracts and options through Goldman Sachs or JP Morgan. The CFO must approve any FX exposure above $100K.

Vendor Payments: Net-30 terms standard. Net-60 requires VP Finance approval. Prepayments exceeding $25K require CFO signature. All vendor contracts above $100K must go through legal review.

Expense Reimbursement: Maximum $500/month per employee without manager approval. Travel expenses require pre-approval for amounts exceeding $2,000. Company credit card limits: $5K for managers, $15K for directors, $50K for VPs.
