NexaFin Technologies - Office Guidelines

Office Hours: The office is open Monday to Friday, 7 AM to 9 PM. Weekend access requires badge approval from your manager. After-hours access is logged for security.

Dress Code: Business casual. No formal suits required. Clean jeans, collared shirts, and sneakers are fine. Client-facing meetings: business professional (advance notice provided). Friday is casual day.

Kitchen and Common Areas: Clean up after yourself - dishes go in the dishwasher, not the sink. Free coffee, tea, and snacks restocked every Monday. Friday lunch is catered by the company. Label your food in the fridge - unlabeled items are discarded every Friday evening.

Meeting Rooms: Book through Google Calendar. 15-minute grace period - unoccupied rooms are released. Standing desks available in rooms 3A and 4B. Video conferencing equipment in all rooms (Zoom Rooms). Phone booths available on each floor for private calls.

Parking: Free parking in the building garage, levels B1 and B2. EV charging stations available on B1 (first come, first served). Bike storage on B2 with showers on the ground floor.

Visitors: All visitors must sign in at reception and wear a visitor badge. Escort required for non-public areas. Client visits should be coordinated with the admin team at least 24 hours in advance.

IT Support: Helpdesk available 9 AM - 6 PM at it-help@nexafin.com or Slack #it-support. Laptop issues: same-day replacement available. New software requests require manager approval via the IT portal.
