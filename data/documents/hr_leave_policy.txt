NexaFin Technologies - Leave and Time-Off Policy

Paid Time Off (PTO): All full-time employees receive 20 days PTO per year, accrued at 1.67 days per month. Unused PTO carries over up to 5 days maximum. PTO beyond the carryover limit is forfeited on December 31st.

Sick Leave: 10 days per year, separate from PTO. Doctor's note required for absences exceeding 3 consecutive days. Unused sick days do not carry over.

Holidays: NexaFin observes 10 company holidays per year including New Year's Day, MLK Day, Presidents Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving (2 days), Christmas Eve, and Christmas Day.

Parental Leave: 16 weeks paid leave for primary caregivers, 8 weeks for secondary caregivers. Applies to birth, adoption, and foster placement. Must be taken within 12 months of the qualifying event.

Bereavement: 5 days for immediate family (spouse, children, parents, siblings). 3 days for extended family (grandparents, in-laws, aunts, uncles).

Leave Requests: Submit through the HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance for planned leave. Emergency leave requires notification to your manager within 24 hours. Requests during blackout periods (quarter-end, product launches) may be denied.

Sabbatical: After 5 years of continuous employment, employees are eligible for a 4-week paid sabbatical. Must be pre-approved 3 months in advance.
